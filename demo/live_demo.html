Step 1: padding

The message is padded to whole 64-byte blocks: a 1 bit (0x80), zeros, then the message length in bits as a 64-bit number.

Steps 2 and 3: message schedule and the 64 rounds

Drag through the rounds. Each round mixes one schedule word W[t] and one constant K[t] into the eight working variables, then shifts them along by one place.

Message schedule W[0..63]

The first 16 words (shaded) are the block itself; the other 48 are mixed from earlier words.

Hash many random inputs (lowercase strings of 8–24 letters) and count how often each pitch and byte value appears. A uniform hash makes every column level.
