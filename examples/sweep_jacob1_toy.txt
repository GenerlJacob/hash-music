Avalanche sweep (toy XOR hash): flip the lowest bit of each input byte in turn
and count the output bits that change. An ideal hash changes about 128 of 256, wherever the
change is.

  byte  char  bits
  1     j     1    #
  2     a     1    #
  3     c     1    #
  4     o     1    #
  5     b     1    #
  6     1     1    #

  Average 1.0 bits (lowest 1, highest 1) over 6 positions.
